## Core Thesis

AI × Web3 is most useful when a product needs both AI's intelligence and Web3's trust layer.

- AI contributes: understanding, generation, automation, personalization, and decision support.
- Web3 contributes: identity, ownership, incentives, verifiable records, payment, and open collaboration.

## Application Scenarios

| Scenario | AI Role | Web3 Role | Why Combination Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decentralized AI infrastructure | Provide model inference, task automation, and data processing. | Provide decentralized compute, storage, data, and resource markets. | AI needs resources; Web3 can coordinate and price decentralized resources. |
| AI Agent + wallet | Understand user goals and automate actions. | Provide wallet identity, asset account, payment, authorization, and execution. | Agents need a way to own identity, pay, transact, and act on-chain. |
| AI on-chain analytics | Summarize and explain complex transaction data. | Provide transparent public transaction records. | Web3 data is open but hard to read; AI turns it into human-readable insight. |
| Smart wallet and security assistant | Explain signatures, detect phishing, and identify risky permissions. | Provide wallet, signature, authorization, smart contract calls, and asset permissions. | AI lowers the safety barrier for normal users entering Web3. |
| Exchange / DeFi risk control | Detect fraud, abnormal transactions, and possible attacks. | Provide transaction, contract, identity, and market risk signals. | AI can monitor patterns while Web3 provides auditable activity data. |

## Problems AI Alone May Not Solve

- Trust: AI can generate or analyze, but it cannot by itself create a shared trust layer.
- Ownership: AI does not naturally manage asset ownership or user-controlled identity.
- Incentives: AI can recommend incentives, but Web3 can encode and distribute them.
- Verification: AI outputs may need verifiable records or provenance.
- Collaboration: Open collaboration may need shared rules and transparent records.
- Payment: Agents need a way to pay, receive, and execute value transfer.

## Problems Web3 Alone May Not Solve

- User understanding: Web3 interfaces are often difficult for normal users.
- Content generation: Web3 does not automatically explain or generate user-facing content.
- Decision support: Chain data is transparent but hard to interpret.
- Automation: Smart contracts execute rules, but do not understand flexible user goals.
- Personalization: Web3 needs AI to create more adaptive user experiences.

## My 5-Sentence Summary

1. AI × Web3 项目可以按能力场景筛选，例如基础设施、Agent 钱包、链上分析、钱包安全和风控。
2. AI 负责理解、分析、生成和自动化，Web3 负责身份、资产、支付、数据和可信执行。
3. AI × Web3 的核心不是发币，而是给 AI Agent 提供经济基础设施。
4. 智能钱包和安全助手是一个典型场景，因为普通用户很难理解签名、授权、交易和风险。
5. 对于新手 Hackathon，我更适合从钱包安全助手、链上数据解释器或 Agent 钱包体验优化这类小场景切入。

## Hackathon Idea Candidate

- Problem: 普通用户在使用 Web3 钱包时，不理解签名、授权、交易风险，容易被钓鱼或误授权。
- Target user: Web3 新手、轻度 crypto 用户、第一次使用 dApp 的用户。
- AI component: 解释交易内容、翻译签名含义、识别钓鱼链接和恶意授权、给出风险提示。
- Web3 component: 钱包身份、链上交易、智能合约调用、资产授权和权限管理。
- Why now: Web3 钱包仍然有很高的安全和理解门槛，AI 可以把复杂交易翻译成普通人能理解的语言。
- Prototype: 做一个“交易前 AI 风险解释器”或“钱包安全 Copilot”的 no-code demo，模拟用户提交交易后获得风险解释和建议。
